Pig Weight Estimator

Estimate live weight from a photo or, better, a short video. Mark a reference object of known length, the body length, and the chest width or depth. The tool converts that to heart girth and applies the farm tape formula weight = girth² × length ÷ 400 (inches → lb), here in metric as girth² × length ÷ 14,400 (cm → kg).

Accuracy depends on the reference sitting in the same plane as the pig, the camera pointing straight at it, and the pig standing square. Lens distortion and a curved back make straight-line length a slight underestimate.

The tape formula is a rule of thumb for finishing pigs; it is less reliable for piglets and heavy sows. Calibrating against one scale weighing corrects systematic bias for your camera setup and breed.
