What gets built

Four kinds of agents, all scoped to your stack — Copilot Studio, Claude, or custom, depending on what you already run.

SUPPORT

Onboarding & support agents

Chat agents for merchants, freelancers, and exchange users — answers questions honestly, escalates what it shouldn't handle, never overstates what the product does.

OPS

Ops & reporting automation

Reconciliation checks, alert triage, dashboards that build themselves — Power Automate, SQL, and Power BI underneath, an agent on top deciding what needs a human.

COMPLY

Compliance triage agents

Walks a small crypto firm through what MiCA or KYC/AML actually requires of them, tracks deadlines, generates the paper trail. Advisory triage, not legal advice.

AGENT

Custom multi-agent builds

Backtesting sandboxes, cross-model market analysis, anything where more than one agent needs to reason and disagree before a human decides.
